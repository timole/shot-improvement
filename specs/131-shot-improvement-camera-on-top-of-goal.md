# 131 — Shot position: camera on top of the goal

New "Ammuntapaikka" choice "Kamera maalin päällä: sinisestä viivasta
päätyyn (22,5 m)" (`blue_line_to_end`): distance from the blue line to the
rink's end line (end boards) = 22.5 m on the IIHF rink (blue lines 15 m
apart, centred in a 60 m rink). Taken literally from the request ("the dist
is from rink end line to blue line"); the plain blue-line-to-goal-line option
(18.5 m) already exists. Files: `core/claps.py`, `tests/test_claps.py`.
